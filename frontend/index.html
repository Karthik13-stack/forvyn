How it works

From a blank page to a finished draft

Every draft follows the same four steps, so you always know what is happening and what to do next.

1

Pick your document

Choose the area of law, then the document you need. You can write it in any of 12 languages.

2

Answer a few questions

Forvyn asks for the details it needs. Type your answers, or speak them.

3

Forvyn finds matching clauses

It looks up the relevant clauses in its template library. If nothing matches, it stops here instead of guessing.

4

Review, sign, download

Edit the draft, add your signature, and download it as a PDF or Word file.

See it work

Every clause comes from a source

Each clause in a draft is matched to a clause template. In this example, the highlighted clause is tied to the Lease Deed template it came from, so you can check where it came from.

If nothing matches, you get a clear “blocked” message instead of text that sounds right but has no source behind it.

FoundMatching clauses found. The draft is written.
BlockedNo matching clause. Nothing is written.

Why you can rely on it

Built to avoid made-up law

Legal text that sounds right but is wrong is worse than no text at all. These are the rules Forvyn follows.

01

It never invents a source

If no matching source is found, Forvyn stops. It does not fall back on general knowledge.

02

Built from clauses, not whole documents

Templates are split into numbered clauses first, so each match is specific and easy to check.

03

You stay in control

Every draft is editable and every risk flag asks for your review. Forvyn helps you draft; it does not give legal advice.
